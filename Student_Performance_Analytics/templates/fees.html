Institutional Banking & Currency Standards

UniTrack AI financial records are accounted in Indian Rupees (INR - ₹) with banking compliance:

  • Main Admin Fee Governance: University Main Administrators have full authority to modify student fee allocations, adjust semester assessments, and extend due dates.
  • Zero Sensitive Card Persistence: Card numbers, CVVs, and banking passwords are never stored in the database.
  • Audited Payment Receipts: Every installment requires an official receipt reference code.
