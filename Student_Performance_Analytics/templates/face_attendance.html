Camera Troubleshooting Guide
If your camera displays a blank or blue screen:
  1. Browser Permission: Click the camera icon or padlock on the left of your browser address bar and select "Allow" for Camera.
  2. App Lockout: Ensure no other software (Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Windows Camera, Skype) is currently using your webcam.
  3. Windows Privacy Settings: Open Windows Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → turn ON "Let desktop apps access your camera".
  4. Secure Origin (HTTPS): Browsers only permit webcam streaming on https:// or localhost.
Zero Hardware Mode: If you don't have a webcam, click "Virtual Demo Camera" or "Upload Photo Scan" to test attendance instantly!
